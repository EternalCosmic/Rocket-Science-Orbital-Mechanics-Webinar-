The Kármán Line: 100 km

Slide 3 • Fundamental Physics (5 Mins)

What is "Space"?

The Kármán Line & Orbital Threshold
  • Proximity: Space is only ~100 km (62 miles) straight up—a 1-hour highway drive vertically.
  • The Kármán Line (100 km): Boundary where thin atmospheric density causes aerodynamic wing lift to vanish.
  • The Space Paradox: Getting to space is easy; staying in space requires extreme tangential velocity to continuously fall around Earth.

Thrust & TVC Control

Slide 4 • Propulsion Dynamics (5 Mins)

Leaving Earth: Newton's Third Law

Action, Reaction, and Vacuum Acceleration
Δp = m · Δv
  • Action & Reaction: Rockets do not push against atmospheric air; they push against their own expelled internal propellant momentum.
  • Nozzle Gas Expansion: High-temperature, high-velocity exhaust pushed downward delivers an equal momentum impulse upward.
  • Thrust Vector Control (TVC): Gimbaling engine nozzles to maintain stability and steer through atmospheric ascent.
🚀

Mass Ratio Fraction

Slide 5 • Rocket Dynamics (5 Mins)

The Tyranny of the Rocket Equation

Tsiolkovsky's Ideal Rocket Equation
Δv = v_e · ln(m_0 / m_f)
  • Logarithmic Mass Trap: Adding propellant linearly increases vehicle mass exponentially.
  • Mass Breakdown: Launch vehicles are ~90% propellant, ~9% dry structure, and only ~1% usable payload.
  • Multistaging: Dropping empty fuel tanks mid-flight reduces dry mass (m_f), boosting acceleration efficiency.

Continuous Free-Fall

Slide 6 • Orbital Physics (7 Mins)

What is an Orbit? (Falling Constantly)

Newton's Cannonball Thought Experiment
  • Gravity in LEO: At 400 km altitude, Earth's gravity is still ~90% of surface gravity.
  • Free-Fall Weightlessness: Spacecraft and astronauts are in continuous horizontal free-fall around Earth.
  • Newton's Cannonball: Firing a projectile at ~7.8 km/s curves its path to match Earth's physical curvature.

Velocity Thresholds

Slide 7 • Velocity Metrics (8 Mins)

The Speed of Space

Circular vs. Escape Velocity
v_c = √((G · M) / r) | v_esc = √2 · v_c
  • LEO Circular Speed (v_c): ~7.67 km/s (~17,500 mph / 28,000 km/h) at 400 km altitude.
  • Orbital Period: Complete planetary revolution every ~92 minutes.
  • Escape Velocity (v_esc): ~11.2 km/s breaks bound Earth orbits into interplanetary trajectories.
